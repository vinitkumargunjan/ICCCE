Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, share, and protect your personal data, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

Last updated: 15 June 2026. This policy applies to the ICCCE 2026 website (www.intcommcon.com) and to data processed in connection with the conference, organised under Joginpally B.R. Engineering College (UGC Autonomous), Hyderabad.

1. Who we are

Data fiduciary

For the purposes of the DPDP Act, the data fiduciary is the ICCCE 2026 organising committee, hosted by Joginpally B.R. Engineering College (UGC Autonomous), Survey No. 156 to 162, Yenkapally Village, Moinabad Mandal, Hyderabad, Telangana 500075, India. You can reach us at iccceconferences@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

Personal data we process

  • Identity & contact data — name, email address, affiliation/institution, country, and any details you provide through the contact form or by email.
  • Submission data — manuscript title, abstract, author details, and the manuscript itself, submitted via the conference submission portal.
  • Registration & payment data — registration category and the information required to process payment. Card/bank details are handled by our payment gateway; we do not store full payment-instrument numbers.
  • Technical data — limited log and security data processed by our hosting and CDN provider (Cloudflare) to deliver and protect the site.
3. Why we use it

Purposes of processing

  • To receive, review, and manage paper submissions and communicate decisions.
  • To process registrations and payments, and issue confirmations and invoices.
  • To prepare accepted, registered, and presented papers for publication, subject to the publisher's approval.
  • To send essential conference communications (deadlines, schedules, logistics).
  • To operate, secure, and improve the website.

We process your data on the basis of your consent and to perform the conference services you request. You may withdraw consent at any time (see section 7); this will not affect processing already carried out.

4. Who we share it with

Recipients and processors

  • Programme committee & reviewers — submission data is shared with reviewers for peer review.
  • Publisher (Springer) — for accepted, registered, and presented papers, the required author and manuscript data is shared with the publisher for proceedings production, subject to the publisher's approval.
  • Payment gateway — to process registration payments securely.
  • Service providers — our submission portal, form-handling service (FormSubmit), and hosting/CDN provider (Cloudflare), acting as data processors on our behalf.

We do not sell your personal data. Some providers may process data outside India; where they do, we rely on the provider's contractual and security safeguards.

5. How long we keep it

Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes above and to meet legal, accounting, and publication requirements. Submission and registration records are retained for the duration of the conference cycle and any associated publication process, after which they are deleted or anonymised unless a longer period is legally required.

6. How we protect it

Security

The site is served over HTTPS and protected by our CDN provider. Access to personal data is limited to organising-committee members and processors who need it. No method of transmission or storage is fully secure; we take reasonable measures to protect your data and to notify you and the Data Protection Board of India of a personal-data breach where required.

7. Your rights

Your rights under the DPDP Act

  • Access a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how it is processed.
  • Request correction, completion, or updating of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Request erasure of your data where it is no longer required.
  • Withdraw consent and nominate another person to exercise your rights in case of incapacity or death.
  • Raise a grievance with us, and escalate to the Data Protection Board of India if unresolved.
8. Grievance contact

How to reach us

To exercise any right or raise a privacy grievance, contact the organising committee at iccceconferences@gmail.com. We aim to respond within a reasonable period as required by applicable law.

9. Changes

Updates to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version, with its "last updated" date, is always available on this page.
