Call for Papers

ICCCE 2026 invites original contributions from researchers, educators, managers, and students across communications, networking, and cyber-physical engineering.

Scope

What we're looking for

Contributions may be conceptual, theoretical, or empirical. They may document research activity, case studies, or best practices that shed light on the theory or practice of engineering, technology, or innovation. Registered authors may present on campus at Joginpally B.R. Engineering College (UGC Autonomous), Hyderabad, or opt for online presentation. The physical conduct of the conference is subject to government guidelines; any change to online-only mode will be intimated in due course.

Approval Pending from Publisher

After peer review and successful presentation, accepted and registered papers are proposed for publication as proceedings with Springer in the “Lecture Notes in Electrical Engineering” series, subject to the publisher's approval. Where approved, select papers will appear in a proceedings book volume. The publisher conducts quality checks on accepted papers; only papers that pass these checks are published.
