Refund & Cancellation Policy

Terms governing cancellation of registration and refund of fees for ICCCE 2026.

Last updated: 15 June 2026. Please read this policy before completing payment. By registering for ICCCE 2026 you agree to these terms.

General principle

Registration fees are non-refundable

Conference registration covers costs that are committed well in advance (venue, publication processing, materials, and organisation). All registration fees, once paid, are completely non-refundable and non-transferable, except in the single case where the conference is cancelled in its entirety by the organisers (see below). A registration fee is linked to a paper and to participation, not to physical attendance — switching to virtual presentation, where permitted, does not entitle you to a refund.

Cancellation by the participant

If you need to cancel

If you cancel your participation, withdraw your paper, fail to attend, or are unable to present for any reason, no refund will be issued. This applies to all registration categories — including author and listener/attendee registrations — regardless of when the cancellation is requested.

Non-refundable charges

Charges that cannot be returned

Bank charges, payment-gateway fees, currency-conversion charges, and applicable government taxes are levied by third parties and are non-refundable in all cases.

Duplicate & erroneous payments

If you were charged twice

If a technical error results in a duplicate or excess payment, the duplicate/excess amount will be refunded to the original payment method after verification. Please report such cases promptly with proof of payment.

Cancellation by the organisers

The only case in which fees are refunded

If the organisers cancel the conference in its entirety, registered participants will be informed and the registration fee will be refunded. The physical conduct of the conference is subject to prevailing government guidelines and may move to an online-only mode; a change of mode is not a cancellation and does not entitle participants to a refund, as the technical programme and publication process continue. Bank, payment-gateway, currency-conversion, and applicable tax charges levied by third parties may not be recoverable even in this case.

How refunds are processed

Processing

Approved refunds are made to the original payment method within a reasonable period after approval, subject to the timelines of the payment gateway and banks involved.
