Refund & Cancellation Policy

Terms governing cancellation of registration and refund of fees for ICCCE 2026.

Last updated: 15 June 2026. Please read this policy before completing payment. By registering for ICCCE 2026 you agree to these terms.

General principle

Registration fees are largely non-refundable

Conference registration covers costs that are committed well in advance (venue, publication processing, materials, and organisation). As a result, fees are generally non-refundable once paid, except as set out below. A registration fee is linked to a paper and to participation, not to physical attendance — switching to virtual presentation, where permitted, does not entitle you to a refund.

Cancellation by the participant

If you need to cancel

  • Author registrations tied to an accepted paper are non-refundable, because acceptance, review, and publication processing have already been carried out. At least one author must register and present for a paper to be included in the proceedings.
  • Listener / attendee registrations may be cancelled by written request to the organisers. Any refund, if approved, is at the organising committee's discretion and will be net of bank, payment-gateway, and applicable tax charges.
  • Refund requests must be made in writing to iccceconferences@gmail.com from the email used at registration, quoting your registration/payment reference.
Non-refundable charges

Charges that cannot be returned

Bank charges, payment-gateway fees, currency-conversion charges, and applicable government taxes are levied by third parties and are non-refundable in all cases, including where a discretionary refund of the base fee is approved.

Duplicate & erroneous payments

If you were charged twice

If a technical error results in a duplicate or excess payment, the duplicate/excess amount will be refunded to the original payment method after verification. Please report such cases promptly with proof of payment.

Changes or cancellation by the organisers

If the conference changes

The physical conduct of the conference is subject to prevailing government guidelines and may move to an online-only mode. A change of mode does not, by itself, entitle participants to a refund, as the technical programme and publication process continue. In the unlikely event that the conference is cancelled in full by the organisers, registered participants will be informed and an appropriate remedy will be communicated.

How refunds are processed

Processing

Approved refunds are made to the original payment method within a reasonable period after approval, subject to the timelines of the payment gateway and banks involved.
