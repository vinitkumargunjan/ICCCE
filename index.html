About the Conference

Where communications research meets cyber-physical systems

ICCCE is an international forum dedicated to the latest developments across networking and communication technology — spanning voice, data, image, and multimedia — and their convergence with cyber-physical systems. Now in its ninth edition, the conference brings together researchers, educators, industry practitioners, and research scholars from around the world.

Across two days, ICCCE 2026 offers high-quality technical sessions, tutorials, and keynotes covering a broad range of perspectives, practices, and technical expertise. Authors may present on campus at Vignan's Institute of Management and Technology for Women, Hyderabad, or online, subject to prevailing guidelines.
